# Platform & Sharing

How progress is shared, stored, and presented outside reading itself.

## Language

**Tracker service**:
An external progress site such as MyAnimeList, AniList, or NovelUpdates.
_Avoid_: Track (for the row), SourceTracker

**Backup**:
A portable archive of library and settings.
_Avoid_: Sync

**Sync**:
A WebDAV or Drive push of progress and selected settings.
_Avoid_: Backup

**Download**:
An offline copy of Chapters.
_Avoid_: Cache

**Statistics**:
Reading sessions, publication status, storage, and heatmap views.
_Avoid_: History

**Updates**:
Date-grouped recent Chapters across the Library.
_Avoid_: History

**Browse**:
The catalog of Sources for discovering Entries.
_Avoid_: Plugin store

**Migration**:
Moving Entries between Sources with their progress.
_Avoid_: Transfer

**Home-screen widget**:
The updates grid shown on the device launcher.
_Avoid_: Widget slot

**Theme**:
A user-chosen color scheme.
_Avoid_: Palette
